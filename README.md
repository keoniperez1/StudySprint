## Step 2b: Evaluate the Top Three

Ratings are preliminary proposals on a 1–5 scale, where 5 is strongest. Scope is rated for ease of completing a useful MVP.

### Idea 1: StudySprint

- **Mobile (4/5):** Mobile notifications and short flashcard sessions make studying convenient anywhere.
- **Story (5/5):** Students need an easy way to organize exams and practice material.
- **Market (5/5):** The app targets college and high school students.
- **Habit (5/5):** Students can use the app daily to review flashcards and track progress.
- **Scope (5/5):** Flashcards, basic quizzes, and reminders are achievable with introductory Android development skills.

**Total: 24/25**

### Idea 2: SplitSnap

- **Mobile (5/5):** Users can quickly record shared expenses immediately after purchases.
- **Story (4/5):** Roommates often need an organized way to track who paid for shared costs.
- **Market (4/5):** Targets students, roommates, and groups sharing expenses.
- **Habit (4/5):** Users return whenever they make shared purchases or settle balances.
- **Scope (4/5):** An MVP can include manual expense entry, group members, and balance calculations.

**Total: 21/25**

### Idea 3: CampusSpot

- **Mobile (5/5):** Location features help users find nearby study spaces while on campus.
- **Story (5/5):** Students may struggle to find quiet, comfortable spaces to study.
- **Market (4/5):** Can serve campus communities, starting with one university.
- **Habit (3/5):** Students may open the app when they need a place to study, but not necessarily every day.
- **Scope (3/5):** Maps, location data, and user reviews may add technical complexity.

**Total: 20/25**

## Step 2c: Final App Decision

**Recommended app:** StudySprint — Smart Study Planner

**Proposed rationale:**

StudySprint offers a useful solution for students who want to organize their coursework and prepare for exams. It also has a manageable development scope for our Android project. We could start with flashcards, quizzes, and a study-task tracker, then add reminders and additional features if time permits.

**Minimum Viable Product (MVP):**
- Create and manage study tasks.
- Create and review flashcards.
- Complete simple multiple-choice quizzes.
- Track study progress.

**Potential future improvements:**
- Study reminders.
- Achievement streaks.
- Shared study sets.
- AI-generated flashcards.

**Final agreed app:** StudySprint

**Reason for final decision:** I chose **StudySprint** because I think it would be a useful app for college students who struggle with managing their study time and preparing for exams. The app would allow users to create flashcards, track study sessions, and organize their assignments in one place.

I also chose this idea because it's realistic to build with the skills I've learned in Android development. I can start with basic features and add more if I have enough time. Overall, I think it's a practical app that students could use regularly.
